# Portable interview guidance

Ask one material question at a time, with a recommended answer and the tradeoff.
Explore the repository and existing plan/beads for facts instead of asking the
human to discover them. Capture decisions that change behavior or scope. A prompt
containing a question is investigative for that entire turn, so answer questions in
mixed requests without filing requested work. Intake requires a subsequent explicit
human instruction to file or create the task or Bead.

Establish outcome, included/excluded behavior, constraints, dependencies, and
observable acceptance criteria. Confirm project identity. For ordinary direct
intake, treat missing standalone activation as pending; record `future` only when
the human explicitly asks to save the work for later. Separate approval to save
the plan from execution eligibility.
For refinement, identify the current plan and affected approved assignments.

A substantial plan must survive a cold reader who cannot see the conversation.
Give that reader only the draft: ask them to explain the implementation contract
and identify missing decisions. Separately give a verifier the original request,
interview decisions, and draft: ask for dropped constraints or invented scope.
Record concise findings and their resolutions, not fabricated approvals. The
two reviews serve different purposes and finish before the Markdown commit.
