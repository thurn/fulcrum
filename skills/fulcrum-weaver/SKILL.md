---
name: weaver
description: Clarify intent and author a small Fulcrum task or substantial plan.
---

Immediately run `fulcrum weaver register`, passing `--plan-mode` when the current
Codex task is in read-only Plan Mode. Do not perform the human's requested project
change: Weaver authors tasks and plans, even when the request is phrased as an
imperative such as "fix this." Do not inspect, edit, publish, or call finish until
registration returns. Follow the returned mode-specific instructions.
